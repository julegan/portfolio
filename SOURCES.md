## PortlandMaps design and later modernization

- **Julie’s firsthand account:** Julie designed the first mobile-first version of PortlandMaps.com more than a decade ago as one contribution to a larger City effort. Do not assign an exact release year or claim she authored the current 2026 interface without further evidence.
- **City of Portland, June 4, 2026:** [A new and improved PortlandMaps.com is coming soon](https://www.portland.gov/bts/cgis/news/2026/6/4/new-and-improved-portlandmaps-coming-soon). The City described the then-current version as released in 2016 and the modernization as primarily a lift-and-shift onto a new framework, not a new creation by its original designer.
- **City of Portland:** [PortlandMaps overview](https://www.portland.gov/bts/cgis/portlandmaps). States the new version was deployed June 24, 2026.

These public sources establish platform chronology and the continuity of the service, but **do not independently establish individual design attribution**. The portfolio's design attribution comes from Julie's firsthand account. The portfolio illustration is conceptual, not an archival screenshot.
